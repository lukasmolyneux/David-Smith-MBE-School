We're building a permanent school for ISF's students in Chbar Ampov, Phnom Penh.

Our goal is to raise $1.35 million USD to buy the land and build a school that will serve generations of students.

Since 2012, ISF has had to move its school twice because of lease and land issues. We don't want our students to have to move again.

For children who have already faced significant disruption in their young lives, another move means adjusting again to a new location, new routines and a new journey to school.

The David Smith MBE School

Why David?

The school is named after David Smith MBE, whose approach to life resonates deeply with ISF.

David first visited ISF in Cambodia in 2024. He spent time in an English class, met our students and played football with them.

David has faced extraordinary challenges in his own life. After seven major surgeries, he has had to learn to walk again and again, including after being paralysed down his left side. His philosophy is simple:

"Be where your feet are"

It means focusing on the present, meeting challenges with optimism and making the most of where you are.

Those values resonate deeply with ISF.

The David Smith MBE School

A School Built For Our Students

Your support can help build a school designed around our students' needs, with purpose-built classrooms, a library, computer room, kitchen and football pitch.

More than just a place to learn, the David Smith MBE School will give our students a safe, permanent place to learn, play and grow, while creating a foundation for generations of students to come.

A Future For Generations

Children join ISF's Catch-Up Programme from around eight years old, having had little or no opportunity to attend school. We help them catch up, return to their local state school and stay in education. And our support continues for more than 10 years, helping students progress towards university, training and stable employment.

Meet Panha*

Panha*, age 10

Before joining ISF, Panha had never been to school because his family couldn't afford to send him.

At ISF, he began learning, playing football and discovering opportunities he had never had before. Today, he dreams of becoming a teacher and a professional football player.

"Before ISF, I did not have any dreams. Now I have such a big one."

A permanent school isn't just about owning a building. It's about giving children like Panha* a secure place to learn and grow for years to come.
